01 / CORE EXPERTISE · MARKETING OPERATIONS & REVENUE ATTRIBUTION

Marketing Operations & Revenue Attribution

How I build closed-loop revenue reporting and measurement infrastructure—connecting top-of-funnel brand awareness, organic search, and digital channels directly to CRM pipeline data, proving C-suite ROI and driving pipeline velocity.

02 / STRATEGIC CHALLENGE

The attribution disconnect

Marketing teams frequently drown in surface-level metrics—clicks, pageviews, and unvalidated form fills—while the C-suite demands accountability on pipeline velocity, customer acquisition cost (CAC), and attributable revenue. When analytics systems operate isolated from CRM and financial data, brand and organic investments are dismissed as unaccountable cost centres, and high-performing growth levers remain chronically underfunded.

03 / METHODOLOGY

The approach

Building closed-loop feedback loops from digital touchpoints to CRM deal stages and revenue.

Closed-Loop Data Flow

Bridging anonymous web interaction streams and digital events directly into CRM pipelines (Salesforce, HubSpot) and enterprise data warehouses (BigQuery).

Multi-Touch Attribution

Deploying advanced attribution frameworks that reveal how top-of-funnel brand visibility and organic touchpoints accelerate closed-won revenue velocity.

Executive BI Cockpits

Translating complex event streams into automated Looker Studio executive dashboards that provide real-time clarity on CAC efficiency, LTV, and pipeline health.

04 / CAPABILITIES

Marketing Operations architecture

Enterprise RevOps infrastructure engineered to connect marketing capital directly to pipeline generation.

Enterprise Data Pipelines & GA4 Architecture

Architecting custom event schemas, server-side tagging, and BigQuery data streaming to maintain high-integrity analytics unaffected by browser restrictions, ad blockers, or cookie deprecation.

CRM Integration & RevOps Alignment

Bi-directional synchronization between digital analytics, marketing automation, and enterprise CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot), ensuring every marketing-generated lead is tracked through opportunity stages to closed revenue.

Multi-Touch Attribution & Pipeline Velocity

Evaluating the cumulative influence of multi-channel interactions across long sales cycles to attribute pipeline value fairly, eliminating channel bias and guiding strategic budget allocation across the leadership team.

C-Suite & Board-Level Reporting

Designing executive BI dashboards that report on commercial KPIs—pipeline generated, blended CAC, customer lifetime value (LTV), and sales velocity—giving CMOs and CFOs undeniable evidence of marketing ROI.

05 / OUTCOMES

Commercial outcomes

Uncompromising capital efficiency and board-level credibility. The ability to defend marketing budgets with closed-loop revenue evidence, eliminate unprofitable ad spend, and scale high-margin organic channels backed by verifiable CRM data.
