04 How I think

Strategic philosophy.

Four core principles that guide how I evaluate problems, design systems and deliver commercial results.

Strategy before channels

Growth starts with prioritisation, not tools: knowing what to do, why, and in what order.

Search as demand intelligence

Organic search reveals how markets think, building compounding acquisition systems rather than temporary spikes.

Measurement as decision infrastructure

Clean analytics, clear attribution, and reporting that enables commercial action rather than passive observation.

Experimentation over shortcuts

Sustainable growth comes from structured testing and evidence, not one-off hacks.
