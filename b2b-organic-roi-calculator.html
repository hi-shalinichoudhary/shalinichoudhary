04 / STRATEGIC PERSPECTIVE

Why RevOps Leaders Track Pipeline, Not Traffic

Pageviews and keyword rankings don't survive CFO scrutiny. When economic pressure tightens, marketing budgets get cut first for teams that report on top-of-funnel vanity metrics instead of commercial impact. Connecting organic search directly to MQLs, SQLs, win rates, and Average Contract Value (ACV) transforms SEO from a content cost center into a predictable, board-level revenue engine.

High-performing growth leaders align search intent with buyer stages, instrument end-to-end CRM attribution, and optimize conversion bottlenecks across every step of the funnel—proving exactly how organic discovery compounds closed-won revenue quarter after quarter.
