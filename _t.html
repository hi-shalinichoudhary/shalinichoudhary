Which AI visibility KPIs were reported?

Brand mention rate, citation count and share, AI share of voice, AI Overview presence, sentiment and AI referral traffic, alongside organic traffic, engagement and conversion.

How was SEO investment defended?

By tracing Salesforce-tracked leads from SEO pages. The annual organic roadmap was signed off by the CBO and marketing director, with weekly briefings to CBO-level leadership.

What changed in the content workflow?

Answer-first passages, FAQ and schema on deep pages, a hub-and-spoke model, and AI-enabled workflows that cut content production time by 40%.

The problem

A migration that could not lose rankings.

Two legacy education brand sites had to move to Drupal without losing search visibility.

Zero ranking loss, under 5% traffic variance, full recovery within one month.

The problem

Low visibility in AI answers.

Ahrefs and SEMrush tests showed low initial AI visibility. Entity signals and a hub-and-spoke model followed.

+15-20% citations on restructured deep content pages.

The problem

Proving what SEO was worth.

Salesforce-tracked leads from SEO pages: 9,800 leads became 3,200 applications in one year.

INR 416 crore roughly, at INR 13 lakh average programme value. Application pipeline, before enrolment conversion.

The problem

One team, one operating model.

Led 5 SEO specialists, 2 content writers and 1 analyst, working with dev, brand and analytics leads.
