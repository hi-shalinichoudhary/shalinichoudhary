Global University Systems · Higher Education · Feb 2022 - Sep 2025

Scaling an INR 416 Crore Application Pipeline from Search Without Paid Amplification

An annual organic roadmap signed off by the CBO and marketing director, leading a team of 7 to 10 specialists, driving 65% organic traffic growth and 3,200 applications.

The situation

Global University Systems required scalable student acquisition across its higher education portfolio without relying on paid amplification. Organic search needed to connect directly to qualified leads and student applications rather than traffic volume alone.

The priority was aligning SEO specialists, content writers, and analytics with development and brand leads under an annual organic roadmap signed off by the CBO and marketing director.

What I did

  • Built an annual organic roadmap signed off by the CBO and marketing director.
  • Led a team of 7 to 10 professionals: 5 SEO specialists, 2 content writers, and 1 analyst.
  • Built the cross-functional operating model with dev, brand, and analytics leads, alongside audit templates, playbooks, and training for stakeholders and junior team members.
  • Reduced content production time by 40% through AI-enabled workflows.
  • Improved lead-to-application conversion by 10 to 12% and reduced bounce rate by 15 to 20%, across 50 to 60K annual leads at roughly 30% lead-to-application conversion.
  • Built Looker Studio executive dashboards and defended SEO investment by tracing Salesforce-tracked leads from SEO pages.

The result

In a single year, SEO pages generated 9,800 leads that converted into 3,200 applications.
