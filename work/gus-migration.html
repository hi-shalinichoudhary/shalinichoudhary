Global University Systems · Enterprise Replatforming & Brand Transition · Feb 2022 - Sep 2025

Protecting Brand Equity & Commercial Pipeline in an Enterprise Multi-Brand Replatform

Directing the high-stakes technical replatform of two flagship higher education institutions to Drupal—orchestrating cross-functional teams to eliminate ranking loss, hold traffic variance under 5%, and protect commercial pipeline with zero revenue downtime.

The challenge

Global University Systems undertook an enterprise technical transformation: migrating two legacy, high-traffic higher education institutional websites onto Drupal. With student recruitment critically dependent on organic discoverability, any indexing breakdown, misconfigured redirect chain, or loss of search visibility would directly disrupt lead generation and threaten a multi-crore student recruitment pipeline.

The central leadership challenge was guiding a complex, multi-team engineering rollout across development leads, brand custodians, regional marketing teams, and analytics stakeholders. The mandate was uncompromising: achieve complete technical modernization on Drupal while guaranteeing zero loss in organic brand equity, uninterrupted recruitment velocity, and seamless user experience.

The strategy

  • Directed the enterprise migration architecture and brand equity preservation strategy across both institutional replatforms at GUS.
  • Established a unified cross-functional operating model uniting engineering sprint leads, brand marketing managers, and analytics specialists, ensuring technical requirements were integrated directly into Jira backlogs.
  • Conducted forensic technical pre-launch audits across thousands of academic URLs: authored 1:1 redirect maps, preserved information architecture hierarchies, and deployed entity-level schema across course directories.
  • Enforced rigorous staging environment protocols: ran automated crawl simulations, validated Core Web Vitals performance benchmarks, and rectified indexing directives prior to production cutover.
  • Maintained direct executive reporting with the Chief Business Officer (CBO) and Marketing Director, providing real-time cutover risk governance, traffic variance tracking, and post-launch visibility assurance.

Business impact

Both legacy education brand websites successfully transitioned to Drupal with zero ranking loss, under 5% traffic variance, and full recovery within one month, entirely safeguarding student recruitment and commercial lead generation.
