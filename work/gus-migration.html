Two Legacy Education Brand Sites Replatformed to Drupal with Zero Ranking Loss

A disciplined engineering collaboration replatforming two core higher education brand websites without losing organic visibility, keeping traffic variance strictly under 5% with full recovery in under one month.

The situation

Global University Systems replatformed two legacy education brand websites onto Drupal. Because student recruitment relies on organic search visibility, any loss in rankings during the migration would directly affect lead generation and student applications.

The transition required close coordination between the SEO team and development, brand, and analytics leads so both education brand sites could move to Drupal without losing search rankings or experiencing traffic drops.

What I did

  • Led the SEO strategy for the Drupal replatform of two legacy education brand sites at GUS.
  • Worked through the cross-functional operating model with development, brand, and analytics leads to align technical SEO requirements with the engineering rollout.
  • Applied standardised audit templates and playbooks across both legacy brand domains prior to and during launch.
  • Tracked rankings and organic traffic variance post-launch through Looker Studio reporting until full recovery was achieved.

The result

Both legacy education brand sites completed the move to Drupal with zero ranking loss, under 5% traffic variance, and full recovery within one month.
