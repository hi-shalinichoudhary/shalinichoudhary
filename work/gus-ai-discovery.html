Reversing AI-Answer Traffic Erosion with Answer-First Content & Entity Signals

How answer-first passage restructuring, hub-and-spoke content architecture, and dedicated AI visibility KPIs drove 15-20% citation gains on restructured deep content pages.

The situation

As AI answers began reducing organic clicks on search results, initial visibility tests in Ahrefs and SEMrush showed low AI visibility across Global University Systems content.

Without stronger entity signals and structured content, generative search features answered queries without citing the brand. GUS needed a clear GEO and AEO response alongside executive reporting to track AI search visibility and defend organic search investment.

What I did

  • Ran AI visibility tests in Ahrefs and SEMrush that identified low initial AI visibility.
  • Restructured deep content pages with answer-first passages, FAQ sections, and schema markup.
  • Built entity signals and a hub-and-spoke content model to strengthen topical authority.
  • Supported off-site authority through Reddit and forum answers, plus genuine review growth on GMB and education portals.
  • Delivered weekly briefings to the marketing director and CBO-level leadership on AI answers reducing organic clicks, and defended SEO investment by tracing Salesforce-tracked leads from SEO pages.
  • Reported AI visibility KPIs: brand mention rate, citation count and share, AI share of voice, AI Overview presence, sentiment, and AI referral traffic, alongside organic traffic, engagement, and conversion.

The result

The restructuring restored discoverability within generative search summaries, capturing early answer citations and securing executive visibility into evolving search behavior.
