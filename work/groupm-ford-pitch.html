Securing the Ford Account Win Through Technical Audits & Market Intelligence

Leading technical website analysis, competitor intelligence, and driving 20% non-branded organic growth at zero budget across global enterprise client portfolios.

The situation

During enterprise agency pitching at GroupM, competitive multi-brand accounts demanded rigorous data-led strategy to validate commercial capability. For the high-stakes Ford account pitch, GroupM needed an in-depth technical search and competitor intelligence assessment that clearly demonstrated organic market opportunities and revenue potential across competitive automotive sectors.

Beyond pitching, existing enterprise clients including PepsiCo and Zee5 required disciplined search governance, demanding proven non-branded organic growth during periods of corporate budget scrutiny. The agency needed to demonstrate that forensic technical search strategies could reliably outperform expensive paid acquisition channels on the same commercial queries.

What I did

  • Owned the technical website analysis and competitor and market analysis that formed the core of the successful Ford pitch.
  • Identified critical technical crawlability bottlenecks, architecture gaps, and indexation inefficiencies across competitive automotive web properties.
  • Led a team of 5 to 6 SEO and digital marketing specialists delivering performance roadmaps across enterprise accounts including Ford, PepsiCo, and Zee5.
  • Engineered zero-budget organic search strategies driving 20% non-branded organic traffic growth, outperforming paid search on identical queries.
  • Managed international SEO programs across multi-regional markets in the Americas and MENA regions.
  • Contributed to pitches, proposals, and scoping, presenting performance data to client stakeholders and building investment rationales when budgets were under scrutiny.

The result

The technical and market analysis directly contributed to securing the Ford account win, while organic initiatives across client portfolios generated compounding traffic without ad spend.
