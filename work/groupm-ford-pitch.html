Securing the Ford Account Win Through Technical Audits & Market Intelligence

Leading technical website analysis, competitor intelligence, and driving 20% non-branded organic growth at zero budget across global enterprise client portfolios.

The situation

At GroupM (WPP Group), winning the Ford pitch required rigorous website technical analysis alongside competitor and market analysis to demonstrate clear organic search opportunity.

Across client accounts including Ford, PepsiCo, and Zee5, teams also needed to deliver non-branded organic growth and present performance data to client stakeholders to justify organic investment when budgets were under scrutiny.

What I did

  • Owned the website technical analysis and the competitor and market analysis that won the Ford pitch.
  • Grew non-branded organic traffic by 20% at zero budget, outperforming paid search on the same queries.
  • Led a team of 5 to 6 SEO and marketing specialists delivering international SEO across the Americas and MENA for clients including Ford, PepsiCo, and Zee5.
  • Contributed to pitches, proposals, and scoping across prospective and existing client accounts.
  • Presented performance data to client stakeholders and built the investment rationale when budgets were under scrutiny.

The result

The technical and market analysis directly contributed to securing the Ford account win, while organic initiatives across client portfolios generated compounding traffic without ad spend.
