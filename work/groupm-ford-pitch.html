Securing the Ford Account Win Through Brand Strategy & Market Intelligence

Directing competitive market intelligence, enterprise brand positioning, and digital acquisition alignment to win the multi-million dollar Ford pitch at GroupM and capture +20% non-branded category share at zero media budget.

The challenge

During a multi-agency global review for the Ford account, the brand faced intense competitive pressure in major automotive categories. Ford's digital acquisition relied heavily on branded navigational search and expensive paid bidding, while competitors were systematically capturing non-branded purchase queries across electric vehicles, commercial fleets, and emerging mobility.

To secure the mandate, GroupM needed to demonstrate how enterprise brand strategy and organic search discovery could align with Ford's high-level automotive goals—repositioning brand perception, defending non-branded market share, and driving dealer inquiries without escalating media spend.

The strategy

  • Directed the competitive intelligence and digital brand architecture workstreams that served as the centerpiece of the winning Ford pitch.
  • Aligned organic acquisition channels with Ford's corporate brand positioning: mapped high-intent category search demand to EV transition milestones and high-margin utility portfolios.
  • Conducted forensic competitor share-of-voice benchmarking against rival OEMs, pinpointing critical content and architecture gaps across regional digital footprints.
  • Led a cross-functional squad of 5 to 6 specialists delivering integrated multi-market digital strategy across the Americas and MENA for global brands including Ford, PepsiCo, and Zee5.
  • Presented executive business cases to C-suite brand and marketing leaders, proving that organic search authority could outperform paid acquisition while permanently protecting market share.

Business impact

The strategic market analysis was decisive in securing the enterprise Ford account for GroupM, demonstrating how strategic search governance transforms digital touchpoints into an enduring brand asset.
