Standardising Multi-Market Digital & Search Architecture Across EMEA, APAC, Americas & MENA

Multi-market SEO on globally indexed domains, implementing entity-level schema, global audit playbooks, and strategy across 10+ multinational client accounts.

The situation

Multi-market organisations operating across several regions need consistent technical standards and structured data across both large global domains and smaller regional sites. At JLL, multi-market SEO across EMEA and APAC ran on a globally indexed domain with thousands of pages alongside regional sites of 100 to 200 pages.

At Ogilvy, delivering SEO across more than 10 accounts in automotive, luxury, healthcare, and e-commerce across the Americas and MENA required repeatable international SEO execution and clear technical contributions to client pitch decks.

What I did

  • Delivered multi-market SEO across EMEA and APAC at JLL on a globally indexed domain.
  • Deployed entity-level schema on sites with thousands of pages and regional sites of 100 to 200 pages at JLL.
  • Created audit templates and playbooks for global regional sites at JLL.
  • Managed SEO across 10+ accounts at Ogilvy in automotive, luxury, healthcare, and e-commerce.
  • Executed international SEO across the Americas and MENA at Ogilvy and authored the SEO sections of client pitch decks.

The result

The playbooks, entity schema systems, and unified audit standards protected multi-market search visibility, establishing durable technical foundations across large global commercial real estate portals and emerging regional consumer websites.
