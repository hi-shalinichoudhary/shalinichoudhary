Standardising Multi-Market SEO Architecture Across EMEA, APAC, Americas & MENA

Multi-market SEO on globally indexed domains, implementing entity-level schema, global audit playbooks, and strategy across 10+ multinational client accounts.

The situation

Global enterprises operating across multiple continents frequently struggle with fragmented domain architectures, inconsistent technical quality, and diluted search equity across localised regional web footprints. At JLL, complex multilingual commercial real estate directories required unified technical governance across EMEA and APAC on a globally indexed domain with thousands of pages.

Similarly at Ogilvy, multinational enterprise accounts in automotive, luxury, healthcare, and e-commerce across the Americas and MENA needed standardised SEO frameworks to maintain organic visibility and indexation hygiene without requiring continuous manual intervention from centralized marketing teams.

What I did

  • Directed multi-market SEO programs across EMEA and APAC on JLL's globally indexed commercial real estate domain.
  • Engineered and deployed entity-level schema architecture across core sites with thousands of pages and regional websites of 100 to 200 pages.
  • Authored repeatable technical audit templates and implementation playbooks for global regional web teams.
  • Delivered international search strategy across 10 or more client accounts at Ogilvy spanning automotive, luxury, healthcare, and e-commerce in the Americas and MENA.
  • Developed technical and strategic SEO sections for enterprise client pitch decks.
  • Built governance playbooks enabling regional digital teams to maintain search visibility independently.

The result

The playbooks, entity schema systems, and unified audit standards protected multi-market search visibility, establishing durable technical foundations across large global commercial real estate portals and emerging regional consumer websites.
