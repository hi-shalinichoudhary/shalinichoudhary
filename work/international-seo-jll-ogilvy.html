JLL & Ogilvy · Global Brand Governance & Multi-Market Strategy · Sep 2016 - Oct 2019

Unifying Global Brand Consistency & Market Penetration Across EMEA, APAC, Americas & MENA

Directing multi-market brand alignment, digital localization governance, and enterprise stakeholder collaboration across 4 continents and 10+ multinational client portfolios at JLL and Ogilvy.

The challenge

Operating across multiple international markets creates acute risks of brand dilution and operational chaos. At JLL, commercial real estate operations spanned globally indexed domains with thousands of pages alongside dozens of regional portals of 100 to 200 pages. Regional digital teams frequently deployed localized content in silos, creating keyword cannibalization, inconsistent brand messaging, and fractured user journeys.

Simultaneously at Ogilvy, managing digital strategies across 10+ multinational client accounts in automotive, luxury, healthcare, and e-commerce across the Americas and MENA required navigating intricate matrixed stakeholder environments. The central leadership challenge was enforcing strict global brand safety and technical consistency while granting regional teams the flexibility required for linguistic nuances and local market penetration.

The strategy

  • Architected a centralized global brand governance framework, authoring standardized audit templates, entity-level schema blueprints, and localization playbooks across EMEA, APAC, the Americas, and MENA.
  • Partnered directly with enterprise stakeholders—including regional marketing directors, corporate brand leads, and localized agency pods—to secure alignment and establish brand compliance protocols.
  • Standardized entity-level structured data across enterprise domains with thousands of pages, ensuring search engines accurately recognized global brand parentage and localized service branches.
  • Resolved complex cross-border hreflang conflicts and domain cannibalization, protecting high-intent organic visibility and preserving localized market share across distinct territories.
  • Authored the strategic digital and organic growth sections of enterprise pitch proposals at Ogilvy, bridging creative brand campaigns with data-backed international acquisition forecasts.

Business impact

The centralized governance models and entity architecture successfully protected brand equity, eliminated cross-market self-competition, and established a scalable foundation for global market expansion.
