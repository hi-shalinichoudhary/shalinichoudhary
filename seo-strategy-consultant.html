02 / METHODOLOGY

The approach

A cohesive framework aligning technical infrastructure, brand authority, generative discovery, and cross-functional governance.

01

Technical search health

Ensuring search bots and answer models can discover, crawl, render, and index core platform infrastructure efficiently. Encompassing crawlability, indexation controls, site architecture, Core Web Vitals, entity-level schema markup, and strategic internal linking graphs.

02

Content and authority

Building authoritative topic hubs mapped to verified searcher intent across the decision journey. Establishing topical authority, semantic entity relevance, and structured hub-and-spoke content models that demonstrate comprehensive subject matter expertise.

03

GEO and AEO (Generative & Answer Engine Optimisation)

Restructuring deep content with answer-first passage models and structured FAQs designed for automated synthesis. Amplifying brand citations, entity co-occurrences, and third-party authority across forums, discussion platforms, and verified review portals.

04

Measurement and decision infrastructure

Connecting organic search activity directly to commercial metrics through Looker Studio executive dashboards and CRM attribution, moving beyond vanity rankings into qualified pipeline contribution and customer acquisition efficiency.

05

Cross-functional operating model

Establishing clear search governance across marketing, content velocity, analytics, product, and engineering teams, ensuring organic search standards and technical requirements are integrated into sprint roadmaps.

03 / AI METRICS

How I measure AI visibility

As search behavior shifts toward conversational answers, tracking classical keyword ranks alone is insufficient. I report a dedicated AI visibility framework alongside organic traffic, engagement, and conversion:

Brand mention rate

Frequency of brand presence across answer engine responses for high-intent queries.

Citation count and share

Total citations earned in generative summaries and market share relative to competitors.

AI share of voice

Proportional prominence within synthetic answers across core commercial topics.

AI Overview presence

Frequency of inclusion in search engine overviews and AI snapshot blocks.

Sentiment

Contextual tone and factual accuracy of brand characterisation within generated outputs.

AI referral traffic

Direct click-through sessions originating from generative engines and answer interfaces.

All AI visibility metrics are integrated alongside organic traffic, user engagement, and Salesforce-tracked lead pipeline to maintain complete commercial transparency.

05 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers on generative discovery, answer engine optimisation, and modern search measurement.

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)? +

Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) is the discipline of optimising digital content and brand entity signals so that generative AI engines, including Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and ChatGPT, discover, parse, and cite your domain in synthetic answers. Unlike traditional SEO which focuses primarily on ranking within blue-link result pages, GEO focuses on structured answer-first passages, semantic entity relevance, and third-party authority that encourage AI models to quote your brand as a trusted source.

How is AEO different from traditional SEO? +

Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO) specifically targets zero-click searches and conversational interfaces that deliver direct answers rather than a list of search links. While classical SEO targets page-level keyword relevance and backlink volume to win top organic SERP positions, AEO structures information into modular question-and-answer formats, concise summary passages, and nested schema markup, making it seamless for both search engines and answer engines to extract and credit your content directly.

How do you measure AI visibility? +

AI visibility is measured through a dedicated KPI framework tracking brand mention rate, citation count and share, AI share of voice, AI Overview presence, sentiment, and AI referral traffic. These metrics are evaluated alongside traditional organic traffic, user engagement, and CRM pipeline attribution to provide leadership with a comprehensive view of organic market presence in both search engines and generative models.
